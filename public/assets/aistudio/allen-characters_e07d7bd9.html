ALLEN Characters

Six characters for AI English Partner. Each one has open and closed eyes, four expressions and a mouth that follows the audio.

Adults
Kids

API: setMouth(id, 0..1) · setEyes(id, "open" | "closed") · setExpression(id, "joy" | "listening" | "thinking" | "talking") · connectAudio(id, audioElement) · connectStream(id, mediaStream) · speak(id, text). Use "all" as id for every character in setEyes and setExpression. IDs: daniel, marcus, laura, sofia, leo, mia. Also works with postMessage({id, level, eyes, expr}).
